## 🧠 Overview

**Neat Video 6** is a professional video noise-reduction and image-restoration plugin designed to improve footage affected by digital noise, film grain, compression artifacts, and other unwanted visual interference. It can be integrated into compatible video editing and compositing applications for detailed restoration work.

The software analyzes the characteristics of source footage and provides precise controls for reducing noise while attempting to preserve important image details such as edges, textures, and fine structures. Its processing tools are useful for footage captured in low-light conditions, high ISO environments, or with cameras that produce noticeable sensor noise.

Neat Video 6 is suited to filmmakers, video editors, colorists, restoration specialists, content creators, and professionals who require cleaner and more consistent footage.

## Additional Information for Windows Users

Neat Video 6 is designed as a specialized restoration tool rather than a complete video editor. It can be inserted into an existing post-production workflow when footage requires additional noise reduction or image cleanup before final delivery.

The noise profiling system helps identify the characteristics of the source material, allowing processing parameters to be adjusted for different cameras, ISO levels, resolutions, and recording conditions. Users can balance filtering intensity with detail preservation to achieve a cleaner image without unnecessarily smoothing important textures.

Temporal and spatial processing approaches can be combined depending on the type of noise present in the footage. GPU acceleration can also reduce processing times on compatible hardware, particularly when working with high-resolution recordings or complex projects.
